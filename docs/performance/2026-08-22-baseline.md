The companion `npm run perf:routes` command performs low-rate, public-route
sampling and writes raw JSON. It is intentionally a smoke benchmark, not a
load test.

## Database evidence

- `openlist_planning_dashboard_aggregate` was the dominant production cost:
  17,764 calls, 26,039,875 ms total, 1,466 ms mean in `pg_stat_statements`.
  Representative executions reached 15–21 seconds and caused 121 Vercel
  statement-timeout errors in the preceding seven days.
- Its unfiltered cold scan read about 430 MB. The production concurrent
  covering index changed the base aggregate scan to an index-only scan, but
  the generic RPC remained too slow because it still materialised and grouped
  the full filtered corpus.
- `openlist_refresh_locality_seo_cohorts()` took 21.2–22.9 seconds while
  repeatedly deriving Planning locality across the corpus. Its narrow
  60-second timeout remains an operational safeguard pending a locality
  summary architecture.
- The three `viewings` RLS auth-initplan findings were corrected with
  `(select auth.uid())` policy expressions, without changing authorization
  semantics.
